## Internal totals

- **Suburb group totals.** Compared in 9,373 region-quarters: the Group Total equals the sum of its suburbs in 7,658 (81.7%) and is never below it. All 1,715 gaps occur where at least one suburb's figure is suppressed, so the total includes leases that aren't published suburb by suburb (largest gap: 28.0% of the total). Use Group Total rows for region-level counts, not a sum of suburbs.
- **LGA totals.** The Victoria row equals the sum of the regional Group Totals in all 742 dwelling-quarters. It equals the sum of the published LGAs in only 77: as with suburbs, suppressed LGAs count towards the totals (every gap has at least one suppressed LGA). The workbook repeats Victoria as Metro + Non-Metro, which matches in 740 and is off by at most 1 letting in the rest; the repeat is dropped.
- **Affordability totals.** The Victoria row equals the sum of the LGAs in all 495 dwelling-quarters. The repeated total at the bottom of each sheet (Metro + Non-Metro) differs by at most 2 lettings or 0.001 of a share (rounding) and is dropped.
