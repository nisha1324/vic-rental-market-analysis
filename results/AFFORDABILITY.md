## The measure

Homes Victoria counts a new lease as **affordable** if its rent is no more than 30% of the income of a lower-income household on Centrelink payments, sized to the number of bedrooms. Table 9 of the September 2025 report gives the households and thresholds (it doesn't show how the threshold is worked out from the income figure, so the thresholds are used as published):

| bedrooms    | household                                       | weekly income   | most it can pay (affordable rent)   |
|:------------|:------------------------------------------------|:----------------|:------------------------------------|
| 1 bedroom   | Singles on JobSeeker                            | $390.55         | $225                                |
| 2 bedrooms  | Single parent on Parenting Payment with 1 child | $728.39         | $345                                |
| 3 bedrooms  | Couple on JobSeeker with 2 children             | $1,083.90       | $450                                |
| 4+ bedrooms | Couple on JobSeeker with 4 children             | $1,347.22       | $545                                |

The thresholds move with Centrelink payments, so the share falls when rents outrun those incomes, not simply when rents rise. Only the latest thresholds are published in the files used here. Every comparison is September with September, from September 2019 (the share is seasonal; December quarters run higher).

## Revisions
